# 04: The plate in daylight

**What to build:** The plate card is the memorable part of light mode. In light mode only the plate card casts a soft contact shadow on the table, and its glaze becomes a light-from-above highlight suited to porcelain (the dark glaze mixes toward ink and would grey the plate). Every other card stays flat. Check the effects that are tuned for dark: the "just added" row tint, the arc pulse (brightening a deep colour may barely show; darkening or a tint step may be needed) and the table grain, if present.
